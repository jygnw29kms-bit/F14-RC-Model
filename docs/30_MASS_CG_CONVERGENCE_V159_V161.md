# Mass and CG convergence v159-v161

The current design is still compatible with the 1050 g target, but the result is a convergence screen rather than a measured ready-to-fly mass.

## v159 mass screen

Current quantified CAD volumes include the v105 lower hardpoint modules, v158 battery tray with interfaces, allowanced inlet walls and the two 6x4 mm / 144 mm carbon cross ties. With provisional XFly EDF pair, the 202 g reference battery and the preferred <=40 g stabilator servo pair, the baseline allocation projects about 971 g in the ASA/light-carbon sensitivity and about 984 g in the PETG/heavy-carbon sensitivity. Hardware, shell mass and material densities are not frozen.

## v160 battery-CG authority

For 1050 g AUW with the 202 g reference battery, the dry airframe should measure approximately X=659.5 to 665.4 mm to cover the complete current preliminary maiden-CG seed range using the proven battery positions X=305 to 375 mm. This is a design target for physical measurement, not a final flight CG.

## v161 equipment trim

Flexible equipment remains a useful no-ballast trim tool. At 848 g dry mass, moving the 90 g ESC + receiver/BEC/wiring allocation by 50 mm changes dry CG by roughly 5.31 mm. Electronics positions therefore remain adjustable until dry-airframe mass and CG are physically measured.

No ballast is to be added as a primary design correction. Move equipment first, then revisit structural/packaging mass if the measured airframe falls outside the achievable range.
