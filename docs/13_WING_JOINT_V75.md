# F-14 wing segment joint v75

v75 converts the v73/v74 fit studies into a production-oriented joint concept without assigning the printed joint the main bending load.

- Joint station: 363.9688 mm model Y.
- Existing inboard rib mating face: 361.768785 mm.
- Existing outboard rib mating face: 366.968785 mm.
- Free rib-to-rib span: 5.2 mm.
- First CAD bridge trial: 5.00 mm long from the outboard rib inward, leaving 0.2 mm axial assembly/bond allowance.
- Skin clearance remains 0.15 mm; carbon/bondline clearance remains 0.10 mm.
- The three carbon bodies remain continuous through the joint and are not cut for the printed connector.

The next CAD gate is a 5.00 mm inboard-projecting bridge generated from the smaller outboard rib profile. It must be checked against both skin segments and all three carbon bodies over the whole insertion path. A full-profile bridge is preferred for torsional area; if taper or carbon geometry prevents the required clearances it will be divided into separated fore/aft keys rather than reducing the carbon clearance.

No print or flight release yet. The P2S clearance coupon under manufacturing/fit_coupons must be printed and measured before freezing the production fit.
