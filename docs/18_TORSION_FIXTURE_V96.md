# F-14 representative torsion fixture — v96

v96 provides a reproducible fixture for the v94 printed joint coupon and the v95 real-carbon specimen.

The fixed clamp is 112 x 18 x 8 mm and the moving clamp is 108 x 18 x 8 mm. Four M4 clearance holes per clamp lie outside the local wing planform. The moving top jaw includes an external lug with two M5.5 lever-bar holes. Broad compliant pads are mandatory.

The fixed clamp spans Y=344.9688…362.9688 mm and the moving clamp Y=364.9688…382.9688 mm, leaving 1 mm on each side of the joint. The minimum M4-hole-edge clearance to the wing planform is >5 mm.

A 20 x 3 x ~200 mm aluminium lever bar produces a true 250 mm arm from the measured spar axis. The sacrificial joint gate is 2.24218 Nm = 8.9687 N at the load line. The eventual non-destructive flight proof is 1.12109 Nm = 4.4844 N.

The fixture is test equipment, not a flight component. Structural release remains blocked until the v93 adhesive process, real-carbon v95 specimen, both-direction SF3 torsion test and subsequent review pass.
