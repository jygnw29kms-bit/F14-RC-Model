# F14 v75 joint-clearance coupon

Purpose: validate the production clearances used by the v73/v74 wing-joint studies on the actual Bambu Lab P2S before releasing the joint geometry.

Test families:
- rectangular slide fits: 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25 mm per-side clearance
- 6 mm rod-hole gauges: 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25 mm radial clearance

Acceptance target for the current CAD candidate:
- 0.15 mm skin/alignment fit must assemble by hand without forcing or visible wall damage.
- 0.10 mm carbon/bondline gauge must not be treated as a press fit; it is a bondline candidate and must allow assembly after printer/material compensation.
- Record actual filament, dried state, nozzle, plate, orientation, measured peg/slot sizes and which clearance is repeatable.

No flight or print release is implied by the coupon geometry alone.
