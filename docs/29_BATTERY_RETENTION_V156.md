# Battery retention v156

The v155 outer packaging gate proves a 196 x 46 x 34 mm structural/service envelope can fit in the registered fuselage without the current wingbox/hardpoint geometry. v156 therefore separates retention functions instead of asking friction or the electrical connector to react crash loads.

- two independent transverse hook-and-loop straps restrain vertical/lateral motion;
- positive cross-pins ahead and behind the pack restrain fore/aft motion;
- 4 mm pin is a packaging candidate only; final material/hardware must pass proof;
- compliant stop shoes distribute pin loads into the battery end face;
- 10 mm indexed pin positions preserve the 70 mm CG adjustment range;
- tray mounts must attach to internal structure, not to shell skin alone.

Hard case is 240 g at 20 g = 47.09 N. Hardware geometry is screened at 2x that force, but actual acceptance remains physical proof with the final printed material and hardware.

The next CAD gate is the skeleton tray and its frame-interface lugs inside the already validated v155 envelope.
