# F-14 center wingbox circular crosstie retention — v99

The independent v98 review correctly rejected crediting the full 360-degree bond area of a 6.0 mm tube inside a 6.4 x 6.4 mm square channel. The side gap was 0.20 mm but the corner gap reached 1.525 mm; only about 22.4% of the circumference would lie within a 0.25 mm radial-gap criterion.

v99 replaces that square channel with a true circular 6.4 mm bore around the 6.0 mm carbon tube. The nominal radial bondline is therefore 0.20 mm around the full circumference. This establishes geometric availability of the full cylindrical interface area, not adhesive/process qualification.

## Verified v99 geometry
- socket span: |Y|=50…74 mm
- actual tube engagement: 22.0 mm
- geometric cylindrical interface area: 414.690 mm² per socket
- fuselage containment: 100% for all four sockets
- all four sockets engage the lower fork structure
- required average interface shear under intended front/rear load share: 1.448 MPa front / 1.363 MPa rear

## Continuous sweep clearance
The previous 2-degree collision sampling was replaced by a 0.5-degree minimum-distance sweep. For every moving-wing/root-lug versus fixed socket/tie pair, v99 records the sampled minimum distance and computes a conservative between-sample movement bound from the moving body's maximum radius about the sweep pivot.

The smallest continuous-clearance lower bound is 36.183 mm, comfortably above the 0.05 mm certification threshold. The v99 retention geometry is therefore certified collision-free over the complete 20–68 degree sweep under this rigid-body geometry model, not merely at the sampled angles.

## Remaining gates
The circular bore makes a uniform nominal bondline geometrically possible, but adhesive fill, surface preparation, real carbon-tube properties, printed/socket material properties and physical retention proof are still open. No mechanical-detail, print or flight release is granted yet.
