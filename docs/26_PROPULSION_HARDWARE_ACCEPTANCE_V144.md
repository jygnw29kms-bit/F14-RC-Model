# Propulsion hardware acceptance v144

The v143 inlet and provisional fan carrier are geometrically valid, but the hardware is deliberately **not frozen** until the exact purchased EDF is measured.

## CAD envelope already proven

- Carrier OD: 55.2 mm.
- Current provisional sleeve ID: 53.0 mm.
- Minimum structural wall target: 0.8 mm.
- Largest sleeve ID possible without enlarging the proven 55.2 mm carrier: 53.6 mm.
- With 0.20 mm radial installation clearance, an EDF shroud up to **53.2 mm OD** can be accommodated by regenerating only the sleeve ID.
- Flow throat remains 50.0 mm.

## Before hardware freeze

Measure the actual unit with calipers. Do not use a nominal “50 mm EDF” label as shroud diameter. Record maximum/minimum shroud OD, body length, lip/ring OD and removability, inlet/outlet ID, mass, motor/lead envelope and any mounting tabs.

A fixed oversized intake lip is unacceptable inside the current constrained nacelle unless a new source-containment review passes. A removable intake ring can simply be omitted where the aircraft inlet duct already provides the inlet geometry.

## Bench gate

Use 2 x 50 A ESCs for the 4S bench article. Log battery sag, per-EDF and total current, installed twin thrust, temperatures and vibration for three 10-second full-power runs. Minimum combined installed thrust target is 1000 g; 1200 g or more is preferred at the intended flight battery state. Total current above 90 A, rubbing, resonance, desync or thermal limiting fails the candidate.

No print or flight release is implied until the measured EDF is fed back into CAD and the installed power system passes the bench gate.
