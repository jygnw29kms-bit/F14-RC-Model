# Propulsion geometry status — v114-v119

The twin-50 mm propulsion path remains viable, but only as a controlled candidate.

## What is now verified analytically

- The QX QF2611 complete 50 mm unit is not a 50–52 mm external package; its published drawing shows an approximately 63 mm outer diameter, so it is rejected for the current aft OML.
- A compact FMS 50 mm housing candidate is screened at approximately 52.5 mm bare shroud OD and 73.5 mm total length. The 60 mm intake lip is not intended to be installed inside the constrained nacelle.
- Station D cannot contain the EDF housing, but after a nominal 0.8 mm duct wall its sampled local duct section still provides about 117% of the conservatively largest screened single-fan FSA. D can therefore remain an intake transition.
- Stations E and F locally pass a 52.5 mm shroud plus 0.8 mm skin and 0.75 mm radial service clearance.
- An exact common lateral center at |Y| ≈ 64.272 mm passes the sampled E and F source polygons, with approximately 0.51 mm and 0.45 mm residual radial margin respectively.
- The relative E→F source registration implies only about 1.41° lateral and 1.37° vertical axis slope.

## What is deliberately NOT claimed

The absolute EDF Z / aircraft waterline is not verified. The historical E/F registration references a source-image SHA-256 that does not match the currently active UPC page image. No assumed crop transform will be used to manufacture an absolute datum.

Therefore no Fusion EDF installation is created yet. The next geometry gate is to recover the exact registration source or independently re-register the aft sections to an active source with a verified aircraft datum. After that, the 73.5 mm continuous shroud envelope and custom inlet/exhaust ducts can be modeled and collision-checked.

Installed thrust, current, voltage sag and ESC temperatures remain physical bench gates.
