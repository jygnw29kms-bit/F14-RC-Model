The centerline-corrected v176 adapter was re-measured natively in Fusion.

- v185 found the right adapter minimum curved-face gap at 0.793458 mm, below the 0.80 mm release screen. Left was 0.801868 mm.
- v186 kept the 50.00 mm inner flow diameter and centerline fixed and scanned only the fan-face outer diameter.
- 51.64 mm is the smallest tested outer diameter that passes bilaterally, with a worst native curved-face gap of 0.802066 mm.
- The correction is only +0.02 mm diameter / +0.01 mm radial material versus v176.

This is a geometry correction basis, not print or flight release. The corrected geometry must be rebuilt and revalidated as a new revision.
