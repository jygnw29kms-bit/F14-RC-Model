# Propulsion hardware verification — v110

The former QX-MOTOR QF2611 50 mm EDF baseline is no longer treated as packaging-compatible hardware.

The official QX technical drawing identifies the complete fan unit as approximately 63 mm outside diameter, 54 mm internal diameter and 39.08 mm housing length. The 50 mm product class therefore does not represent the external installation diameter.

The imported v51 aft-section sensitivity had assumed a 52 mm housing and found local available diameters of roughly 50.17 mm at D, 57.63 mm at E and 58.99 mm at F. A verified 63 mm shroud exceeds all three. QF2611 remains a performance benchmark only until a new aft installation proves otherwise.

Next propulsion gates:

1. Re-register the **current** aft nacelle/OML geometry.
2. Determine the usable axial interval and whether the EDF can start aft of the D bottleneck.
3. Screen replacement EDFs using verified **external shroud OD and length**, not nominal rotor diameter.
4. Only after packaging freeze: bench installed thrust/current/voltage sag and ESC cooling.

No propulsion, print or flight release is implied.
