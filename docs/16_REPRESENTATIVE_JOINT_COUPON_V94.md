# F-14 representative joint coupon — v94

v94 combines the final v89 insert/inner-side geometry with the matching v82 outer wing skin and crops a 40 mm local specimen about the true joint station Y=363.9688 mm.

## Printed bodies
Four separate STL files are exported: inner skin, outer skin, final v89 insert/outboard-rib body, and inner rib. This reproduces the actual printed interfaces instead of replacing the joint with a simplified flat specimen.

## CAD fit
- insert / inner skin: 0.15634 mm
- insert / outer skin: 0.13837 mm
- insert / inner rib: 0.22001 mm
- insert / carbon minimum: 0.10785 mm
- inner / outer skin segment boundary: 0 mm axial gap

The three carbon reference bodies remain continuous across all 40 mm of the coupon. The coordinates from the v82 and v89 lineages therefore combine without a detected placement mismatch.

## Test status
v94 is a printable geometry/fit specimen and a basis for the representative bond test. The carbon bodies are references only at this stage; the coupon is not yet a full structural test specimen. v95 must turn those references into a practical CFK fabrication and torque-fixture plan.

The structural target remains 2.24218 Nm for a sacrificial full representative joint and 1.12109 Nm non-destructive proof for the eventual flight article. No structural or flight release is granted by v94 alone.
