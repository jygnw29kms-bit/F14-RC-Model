# CG and battery integration v150-v152

The moving outer-wing aerodynamic-center screen is not a flight-CG calculation. v151 therefore combines it with broad fixed-glove/body and tail-effectiveness sensitivities and keeps the result as a neutral-point envelope, not a painted CG mark. The current conservative maiden-CG sensitivity is roughly x=596-605 mm in the project model frame; this remains provisional.

The battery tray is intentionally given **70 mm of usable battery-center travel**. A 202 g reference pack then provides about 13.5 mm CG authority at 1050 g AUW and 11.8 mm at the 1200 g redesign threshold. This is preferred to ballast.

The reference 4S 1800 mAh pack is not frozen. Final capacity is selected from installed EDF current, voltage sag, mass and flight-time tests. Battery mass target remains about 200 g with a 240 g hard warning. The tray uses positive fore/aft stops and structural strap anchors sized for a 20 g retention proof (47.1 N for a 240 g pack).
